Kenney CC0 assets. The game loads these by default. Open with ?classic=1 for the procedural court.

Used
- Graveyard Kit 5.0 (CC0) — Kenney
  https://kenney.nl/assets/graveyard-kit
  Characters: character-zombie (slum wolf), character-ghost (hedge witch).
  The hero is the KayKit Adventurers mage and the ghoul is the KayKit skeleton minion. See CREDITS.md.
  These graveyard rigs include idle / walk / sprint.
  Props: fence, fence-damaged, iron-fence, lantern-candle, debris, debris-wood, rocks.
  License: graveyard-kit/License.txt

- Blocky Characters 2.0 (CC0) — Kenney

Gaps
- No dumpster-branded trash bag or true chain-link roll. Trash is a food-kit bag plus cans. Fences are Retro Urban wall-fence (bars) and City Kit construction-fence. Fire escapes are balcony ladders, a platformer ladder, and scaffolding, not a full switchback stair.
- No suited human with a modeled fedora. Blocky Characters supply the suits and a walk cycle; the hat and glasses are extra meshes on that rig.
- Animated Characters packs are still FBX without a walk clip, so they are not used.
