This folder holds the complete free Rig_Medium animation sets as Med_*.glb
(clip names keep the Rig_Medium| prefix). Mesh-stripped GLB re-exports of the
official free KayKit Character Animations pack.

Blood-hunt runtime loaders use the cleaned siblings one level up:
  ../anim-combat.glb ../anim-general.glb ../anim-move.glb
plus the additional free categories also cleaned at the parent folder:
  ../anim-ranged.glb ../anim-move-advanced.glb ../anim-simulation.glb
  ../anim-special.glb ../anim-tools.glb
(those strip the Rig_Medium| prefix so clip names match Idle_A / Walking_A /
Melee_1H_Attack_Stab, etc.)

Characters: ../mage.glb (Adventurers free Mage) and ../skeleton-minion.glb.

Not included: paid SOURCE/Extra Blender files.
Kenney Animated Characters — researched, not shipped (FBX, no usable walk clip).
Mixamo — none free/licensed found in workspace.
Quaternius on disk — props/buildings/rat only; no character anim library.
